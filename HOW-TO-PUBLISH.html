How to Publish Your Website

Pawleys Island Surf Cam  ·  Step-by-step hosting guide

Your Website Files

You have three files that belong together in the same folder:

📄  pawleys-island-surf-cam.html    ← Main website

📄  links.html                         ← Your links page

📄  _redirects                         ← Netlify routing helper

Keep all three together — never separate them. Everything else (photos, tide data, fonts) loads automatically from the internet.

Option 1 — Netlify Drop (Easiest — Free, 2 minutes)

Recommended

Step 1 — Put your files in one folder

Create a folder on your desktop called Pawleys Surf Cam and place all three files inside it.

Step 2 — Go to Netlify Drop

Open your browser and go to: app.netlify.com/drop

You don't need to create an account first — you can sign up after.

Step 3 — Drag & Drop

Drag your Pawleys Surf Cam folder directly onto the Netlify Drop page. It will upload in seconds.

Step 4 — You're live!

Netlify gives you a free URL like sparkling-sundae-abc123.netlify.app. You can share this link with anyone immediately.

Step 5 — Set a custom domain (optional)

In your Netlify dashboard: Site settings → Domain management → Add a custom domain. If you own a domain like pawleyssurfcam.com, you can connect it here. Domains typically cost $10–15/year from GoDaddy, Namecheap, or Google Domains.

Free tier limits: Netlify's free plan includes 100GB of bandwidth per month and unlimited deploys — more than enough for a site like this.

Option 2 — GitHub Pages (Free, technical)

Free
  1. Create a free account at github.com
  2. Create a new repository (e.g. pawleys-surf-cam)
  3. Upload your three files to the repository
  4. Go to Settings → Pages → Source → main branch
  5. Your site will be live at yourusername.github.io/pawleys-surf-cam

Rename pawleys-island-surf-cam.html to index.html first so it loads as the homepage automatically.

Option 3 — Cloudflare Pages (Free, fast)

Free · Fastest CDN
  1. Create an account at pages.cloudflare.com
  2. Click Create a project → Direct Upload
  3. Upload your three files
  4. Your site is live instantly on Cloudflare's global network

Cloudflare Pages has unlimited bandwidth on the free tier and loads very fast worldwide.

Adding Your Live Stream

When you have a live camera feed (YouTube Live, Axis camera, Wowza, etc.), open pawleys-island-surf-cam.html in a text editor and find this comment around line 1650:

╔══ PLACEHOLDER — swap with your live stream iframe ══╗

Replace the placeholder block with an <iframe> tag pointing to your stream URL. Example for YouTube Live:

<iframe

  style="width:100%;height:100%;border:none"

  src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/YOUR_STREAM_ID?autoplay=1&mute=1"

  allow="autoplay; encrypted-media" allowfullscreen>

</iframe>

Then re-upload the updated file to Netlify (just drag again — it replaces automatically).
